## Mass properties

Every machined and printed part on this site that is in the Fusion model has one row in
[part-properties.csv](../data/part-properties.csv){ download="" }, generated by `tools/gen_part_properties.py`
from the CAD export (`tree.csv`, Fusion's own mass properties of every component) and never hand-edited. The
**Mass / size** column on [CNC parts](../bom/cnc-parts.md) and [Printed parts](../bom/printed-parts.md) is read
from it. Every value is CAD-derived; nothing is measured on a built part.

{{ pp|selectattr("bbox_x_mm")|list|length }}, centre of mass for {{ pp|selectattr("com_x_mm")|list|length }},
inertia about the centre of mass for {{ pp|selectattr("ixx")|list|length }} (about the component origin for
{{ pp|selectattr("ixx_origin")|list|length }}), volume for {{ pp|selectattr("volume_cm3")|list|length }}.

| Column | Unit | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mass_g` | g | Fusion, with the material assigned in CAD |
| `bbox_x_mm`, `bbox_y_mm`, `bbox_z_mm` | mm | axis-aligned bounding box, Fusion (from the STL where `notes` says so) |
| `com_x_mm`, `com_y_mm`, `com_z_mm` | mm | centre of mass, Fusion (from the STL where `notes` says so) |
| `ixx`, `iyy`, `izz`, `ixy`, `ixz`, `iyz` | g·mm² | inertia matrix about the centre of mass: Fusion's tensor about the component origin, moved to the centre of mass with the parallel-axis theorem |
| `ixx_origin` … `iyz_origin` | g·mm² | Fusion's inertia matrix about the component-frame origin, unchanged |
| `volume_cm3` | cm³ | the part's STL (closed mesh); the one mesh-derived column |
| `material`, `fusion_name`, `notes` | — | Fusion material name, Fusion component, reason for any blank or doubt |

**Frame.** Bounding box, centre of mass and both inertia matrices are in the part's own coordinate frame, the
frame of its STEP and STL downloads, not the robot frame. The matrices are axis-aligned with that frame; the six
values are their entries, off-diagonals in tensor form (`ixy` = −∫ x y dm), as a URDF `<inertia>` tag takes them.
Divide by 10⁹ for kg·m².

**Precision.** The export carries each mass to 0.1 g. Moving the origin-referenced matrix to the centre of mass
multiplies that rounding by the square of the distance between the two, so `notes` states the bound
(±0.05 g × distance²) wherever it exceeds 2 % of the largest moment, and the six centre-of-mass values are left
blank where it exceeds the moments themselves. The `*_origin` columns are exact in every row.

!!! unverified "UNVERIFIED — inertia about the centre of mass for every part whose `notes` give a ± bound: the export rounds the mass to 0.1 g and these parts sit far from their component origin"
    The `*_origin` columns are Fusion's exact values; the bound is stated per row. A re-export with the mass to
    0.001 g (or the matrix taken about the centre of mass inside Fusion) removes it.
    *Owner: hardware lead, from the CAD (`tools/fusion_export/`).*
