# Before you start

Read [Safety](safety.md) first.

## Go / no-go

- [ ] Your safety office has read [Safety](safety.md).
- [ ] A second person is available for every lift and powered test.
- [ ] A gantry or hoist rated 50 kg or more, with 1.4 m or more of clear height and clear floor beneath.
- [ ] Written lithium-polymer charging, storage and fire procedures exist.
- [ ] An emergency stop is reachable from outside the working envelope. None in the bill of materials (BOM): **TODO**{ .dh-missing }.
- [ ] You can have parts machined and inspect them. No tolerances published: **TODO**{ .dh-missing }.
- [ ] You have FDM (fused deposition modelling) printing, and SLS (selective laser sintering) printing or a service bureau.
- [ ] You can solder XT30 and crimp signal connectors.
- [ ] You have quotes and dates for long-lead items.
