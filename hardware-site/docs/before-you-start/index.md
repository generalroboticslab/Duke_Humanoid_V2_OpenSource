# Before you start

Read [Safety](#safety) first.

## Go / no-go

- [ ] Your safety office has read [Safety](#safety).
- [ ] A second person is available for every lift and powered test.
- [ ] A gantry or hoist rated 50 kg or more, with 1.4 m or more of clear height and clear floor beneath.
- [ ] Written lithium-polymer charging, storage and fire procedures exist.
- [ ] Every operator knows the three software stop layers and who triggers each ([Safety](#safety)); the robot has no hand e-stop.
- [ ] You can have parts machined and inspect them.
- [ ] You have FDM (fused deposition modelling) printing, and SLS (selective laser sintering) printing or a service bureau.
- [ ] You can solder XT30 and crimp signal connectors.
- [ ] You have quotes and dates for long-lead items.
- [ ] You have downloaded and checksummed one release tag of the CAD ([CAD downloads](../fabrication/index.md#cad-downloads)); it is Apache-2.0 ([Citation and licence](../reference/index.md#citation-and-licence)).
- [ ] No unpriced BOM rows in what you buy ([BOM](../bom/index.md)).
